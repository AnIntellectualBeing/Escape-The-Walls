1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how An Intellectual Company ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the iPhone and Android game Escape the Walls (the "Game"). By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information processed

Information we directly collect

The current version of Escape the Walls does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment-card information. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to include so we can respond to your request.

Information stored locally

Game progress, unlocked levels, scores, preferences, and settings may be stored locally on your device. This local information may be removed if you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Depending on your iCloud, Android or Google backup settings, the operating system may back up some app data on your behalf.

Advertising information

Escape the Walls uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • app launches, ad views, taps, and other app or ad interactions;
  • device, operating-system, and app information; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

On iPhone, the Game asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads can still appear but are not personalized using the iPhone advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, age treatment, and applicable law. Google's handling of information is governed by its own policies.

3. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • operate and maintain the Game;
  • save progress and preferences on your device;
  • display, measure, and improve advertisements;
  • understand app performance and diagnose crashes or technical problems;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell information that you directly provide to us. Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to operate the Game, deliver advertisements, provide analytics or diagnostics, prevent fraud, comply with law, or protect users and our rights.

The principal third-party services are Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and, for iPhone purchases, RevenueCat (privacy policy). Google may process information on servers located outside your country and according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or turn off tracking on iPhone, through your device's privacy settings.
  • You can manage Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.
  • You can limit certain data processing through consent or privacy controls shown where required.
  • You can remove locally stored Game data by clearing app data or uninstalling the Game.
  • You may contact us to ask a privacy question or request action concerning information you provided directly to us.

Some information is processed by Google rather than directly controlled by us. Requests concerning Google's data should be made using Google's privacy controls and request mechanisms.

6. Children's privacy

Escape the Walls is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information directly to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address the request. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of apps and device privacy settings.

7. Retention and security

Locally stored Game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the Game is uninstalled, subject to any device backup settings. Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, protect our rights, and comply with legal obligations. Third-party providers determine their own retention periods under their policies.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Game and rely on service providers' security controls. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. In-app purchases

On iPhone, Escape the Walls offers optional in-app purchases such as Shards, the Starter Pack, the Neon Pack and Remove Ads. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; An Intellectual Company does not receive your card details or Apple ID. To unlock and restore purchases, the Game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier, the products purchased and Apple's purchase receipt information. It does not receive your name or email address from us. Purchases are optional and the Game can be played without buying anything.

The Android version currently has no purchases. If they are added there, they will be processed through Google Play Billing and this policy will be updated.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated within the Game or through the App Store or Google Play listing where appropriate.
